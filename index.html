Our Philosophy

A Story of Passion
& Provenance

EMBER & VINE was born from a singular obsession: the perfect marriage of fire-kissed American ingredients and refined culinary technique. Our kitchen celebrates the seasons, honors local farmers, and pushes the boundaries of modern American cuisine.

Every plate that leaves our pass is a conversation between tradition and imagination — rooted in the flavors of the Midwest, elevated by the creativity of our brigade.

The Mind Behind the Menu

Chef Marcus Rivera

Chef Marcus Rivera grew up in New Orleans, where French technique and American soul food created a lifelong obsession. After training under Alain Ducasse in Paris and Eric Ripert in New York, he returned to the Midwest to build something entirely his own.

"Every ingredient carries a story. My job is to tell it as honestly and beautifully as I can."

At Ember & Vine, Rivera has built a kitchen rooted in curiosity, deep respect for the supply chain, and a relentless pursuit of flavor that has earned him national recognition.
